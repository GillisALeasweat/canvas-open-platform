# THE CANVAS
## Next-Generation Modular Smartphone Open Platform
### Original Design Draft (v5 - Open Discussion Edition)

> "CANVAS is not merely an attempt to redesign the smartphone, but to redesign the industrial structure centered around it."

## Overview
This repository contains the integrated grand design of THE CANVAS, outlining its fundamental principles, architecture, operational philosophy, and market significance. Rather than presenting final mass-production specifications, this draft establishes a solid backbone to enable engineers, enterprises, and researchers to concretize and validate the platform.

## Key Principles
- **Separation of Core & Specialized Gadgets**: Decoupling the high-performance base computing terminal from specialized vendor gadgets.
- **Open Standards**: Ensuring free market entry without breaking legacy products unnecessarily.
- **Long-Term Value**: Supporting repair, replacement, upgrades, and rebuilding to maintain product lifespan.
